The challenge

Baker Accounting Services was building a new firm with deep CPA and private-equity experience—but no brand system to communicate credibility, clarity, and the rare combination of financial advisory plus HR support under one roof.

The solution

I designed a clean navy-and-steel identity with a geometric diamond mark, professional stationery, and social assets. The system feels institutional and approachable—ready for client-facing materials as the firm grows.
