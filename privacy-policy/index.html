Privacy policy

Noble Marketing & Design (“Noble,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use thenoblemarketing.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Noble Marketing & Design is a boutique branding, website, and social media studio based in Lititz, Pennsylvania. Questions about this policy: audrey@thenoblemarketing.com.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to share and limited technical data that helps the Site work.

  • Contact details. When you email us, book a discovery call, or submit a form, we receive information such as your name, email address, business name, phone number (if provided), and the message you send.
  • Project context. Details you share about your brand, goals, timeline, or current assets so we can respond thoughtfully.
  • Usage data. Standard log and analytics information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, browser type, device type, and referring URL.
  • Cookies and similar tools. We may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and site performance (for example Google Analytics). You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery calls
  • Provide and improve our branding, website, and social media services
  • Send project-related communications you request
  • Understand how the Site is used so we can improve content and performance
  • Protect the Site, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the Site or run the studio (for example email, hosting, analytics, or scheduling tools). Those providers are expected to use the information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users, or in connection with a business transfer.

Data retention

We keep inquiry and project-related information for as long as needed to respond, fulfill services, maintain business records, or meet legal requirements. Analytics data is retained according to the settings of the tools we use.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You may email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.
  • You can opt out of non-essential cookies through your browser.
  • If you no longer want marketing emails from us (if we send any), use the unsubscribe link or email us directly.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party links

The Site may link to third-party websites or platforms (including client sites and social networks). Their privacy practices are their own; we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
