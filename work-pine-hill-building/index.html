About the client

Pine Hill Building Company is a hometown custom home builder in Lititz, Pennsylvania. On phbchomes.com, they describe the company as a collaboration of experience, passion, and belief, with more than 80 years of combined home building across Central Pennsylvania. They stay with each client from choosing a plan and a lot through the day they hand over the keys. Their promise on the site is direct: when you build with Pine Hill, you become family.

The challenge

Word of mouth was already bringing Pine Hill strong clients. They lacked a current social media strategy to showcase finished projects and the skill behind them, and to introduce people from outside the area to their custom home building process.

The solution

Noble created a social media strategy that keeps Pine Hill relevant and top of mind, so they can compete with other builders and send more leads to their website.
