About the client

By Anna Claire is Anna Claire, a lifestyle and wedding photographer based in Central Virginia. She photographs weddings, elopements, engagements, and portraits, with an eye for the details people usually miss. She stays with clients through the long arc: senior pictures, the walk down the aisle, and family sessions years later.

The challenge

Her photographs already tell a clear story on her website. The feed still needs a steady rhythm that feels like her: film, quiet details, and the moments between the ceremony, so couples in Central Virginia meet that same voice on Instagram.

The solution

Social support built from the work she already makes: her portraits, wedding and engagement frames, and captions in her voice.
