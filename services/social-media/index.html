Starter Presence Package — consistent presence without heavy production

An efficient social media solution for small businesses that want to maintain a consistent, professional presence without ongoing strategy meetings or heavy content production.

Foundational Presence

Foundational Social Presence Package — visibility with a monthly strategy touchpoint

Best for small businesses looking to stay visible and on-brand without full content production.

  • 8 posts/month
  • On-brand post copy

Growth Presence

Growth Social Presence Package — higher volume + video mix

A comprehensive social media solution designed for brands ready to grow—focused on higher content volume, video visibility, audience engagement, and performance insights.

Premier Social Presence Package — maximum cadence with reels emphasis

Brands ready to elevate their social media presence by incorporating video content for increased reach, while still prioritizing strong visual consistency through curated photography and branded graphics — growth without sacrificing polish.

  • Scheduling and go-live support
