About the Client

32° Below Ice Cream Shop is a family-owned business that loves serving the Lititz community and making memories—one scoop at a time.

The challenge

With a rebrand complete and a second downtown Lititz location open, 32° Below needed social content that could keep pace—seasonal hours at Bricker Village, year-round posts for Main Street, flavor drops, and family-friendly events without sounding like two different businesses.

The solution

Noble supports their social media so the shop stays top of mind for locals and visitors: clear location cues, on-brand visuals, and a steady feed that matches the warmth guests feel at the counter—one family, two Lititz stops, same creamery-quality scoops.
