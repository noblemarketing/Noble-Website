32° Below — 90K views, $0 ads

A local scoop shop reached 18,237 unique people in under five months. Entirely organic. That is the number that actually means someone found them.

Ice cream does not need a brand lecture. It needs a feed that makes you want the next scoop—flavor of the month, the family at the counter, the sign you drive past in Lititz. For 32° Below, the work was to show up like that every week, without buying the audience.

In under five months, that rhythm produced 90K+ organic views and a 348% increase in Facebook reach. Eighteen thousand unique people saw the shop. None of that was paid.

01 Views are nice. Unique people are the point.

Facebook views jumped 332% to about 59K—the loudest number in the report. I still look first at reach. A 348% increase in viewers means the content left the usual circle. Someone who was not already following saw a pretzel sundae, a wedding pint, a chalkboard special, and remembered the name.

Zero dollars in ads. That is not a constraint. It is the strategy.

02 Same voice on both platforms

Instagram reach climbed 82% to 10.8K. Interactions—likes, comments, shares—were up 123–196% depending on the channel. That range matters: people were not just seeing the posts. They were answering them.

The creative stayed close to the counter. Monthly flavors, event pints, the dipping cabinet at golden hour. Nothing that could have been made for a generic dessert brand in another town.

The result

90K+ views and 18,237 unique people in under five months—a local scoop shop found, not bought.
